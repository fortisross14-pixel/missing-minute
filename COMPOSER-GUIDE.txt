MARA QUILBLE — SCENE COMPOSER QUICK GUIDE
==========================================

1. Apply this ZIP over the current project root.
2. Run the game normally.
3. Click “Scene composer” in the header.
4. Select Office or Harbor from the composer panel.
5. Drag a visual object directly in the scene.
6. Drag the yellow square at the lower-right corner to resize it.
7. Use the numeric fields for precise X, Y, width, height, Z-index and opacity.
8. Arrow keys move the selected element by 1 pixel.
9. Shift + arrow moves it by 10 pixels.
10. “Move matching hotspot” keeps the object’s interaction area aligned.
11. Hotspots can also be selected and edited independently from the dropdown.
12. Use the camera slider at the bottom of the room to inspect a wide scene.
13. Export JSON when the composition is ready.
14. Upload that JSON in the next chat turn.

The exported file is named:

  <scene-id>-composition.json

Scene composition data is stored separately from the game save. Pressing Restart does
not delete composition work. Use “Reset scene” inside the composer only when you want
to discard a room’s layout edits.

State variants move together where appropriate, including:
- office clock today/tomorrow
- office calendar today/tomorrow
- office alarm broken/repaired
- Captain Nib without/with hat
- harbor pump with/without funnel
- harbor sign hanging/fallen
